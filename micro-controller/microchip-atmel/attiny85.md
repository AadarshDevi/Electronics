# ATTiny85

## Parts
1. ATTiny85-20PU
2. Jumper Wires
3. Breadboard

## Connection

### CP2102 USB to TTL Adaptor Programmer
| USB | ATTiny85 | Wire |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 3V3 | VCC | Light Brown/Red |
| GND | GND | Black |
| RX | Pin 7 | Orange |
| TX | Pin 3 | Yellow |
